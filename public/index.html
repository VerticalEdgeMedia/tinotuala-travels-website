Moments, not itineraries

Nobody gets home and talks about the itinerary.

They talk about the ten minutes nobody planned. Here is the sort of thing a Tino trip is built to leave you with.

Krabi

The engine cuts and the longtail slides onto the sand. For about a minute the loudest thing in Thailand is water coming off a cliff.

Phuket

Shophouse pink, a cold drink, the afternoon rain arriving sideways. Nobody moves, and nobody minds.

Bangkok

Flame at head height, a plastic stool at knee height. You point at something, eat it, then point at it again.

Scroll sideways

Tokyo

Six seats, one chef, a door you would have walked straight past. Two hours later you know everyone's name.

Mykonos

Quad bike, sun on your forearms, straight over the middle of the island because somebody said the bakery there is worth it. It was.

Barcelona

Eight of you around one long table in the Gothic Quarter, three floors up, the last plate arriving somewhere near midnight.

How it works

Four steps, and one of them is the holiday.

  1. I

    A chat

    Where you are drawn to, who is coming, how you like to spend a day. Tino asks a lot of questions, and the answers are the brief.

  2. II

    A trip, designed

    Built out of your answers: where you stay, how you move between places, what is worth locking in early and what is much better left open.

  3. III

    You go

    With room left in the schedule, so one good tip from someone who lives there can change the whole afternoon.

  4. IV

    Tino stays in the loop

    Plans move. Weather turns. Someone falls for a town and wants two more nights in it. He would much rather you asked.

About

Hi! I'm Tino.

I'm a vibe booster and citizen of the world! I LOVE encouraging everybody to travel. I believe it's where life's greatest memories are made.

Tell me how you like to spend a day, who you're bringing and what you never want to do again, and I'll design the trip around that. Proper time with the locals, room in the schedule to follow your nose, and zero cattle sightseeing. Got a place in mind, or just a feeling? Hit me up!
